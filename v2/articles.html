MCAST Canteen Reviews

According to the results of our survey, a significant number of students expressed concerns regarding the management and overall quality of the canteen services at the MCAST Mosta campus. The feedback collected highlighted several areas that students believe could be improved, including the quality and variety of food, customer service, cleanliness, pricing, and the overall dining experience. These concerns were gathered from students across the different MCAST campuses to provide a broader understanding of their experiences and expectations. The following section presents a summary of the main comments and opinions shared by students through the survey, highlighting the key issues identified.

Canteen Rating: 0/5

  • Horrible experience.
  • It is too small.
  • Food is overpriced.
  • The food is oily.
